FAQ

The questions traders ask.

Why would I join?
You can already tell people you are a good trader. JYM lets you prove it, so they don't have to believe you.

Trade your own account. Same rules as everyone else. We read the public page, like everyone. Finish in the top five and your name goes on the record.
Do I have to wait three months to show anything?
No. The top five board is public through the quarter, so if you reach it, anyone can see you there before the end.

If you drop off it, nothing is announced.
What do I actually get?
A verified record of three months of your trading.

Finish in the top five and your name stays permanently attached to that record, with your own links on it. First place also gets a physical trophy.

That is the whole of it. We do not promise you an audience.
What if my three months go badly?
Nothing is published. Only the top five are named. If you don't place, no result, no rank and no number ever appears.

If you do place and would rather not be named, just tell us and we leave your name off. Nobody is published against their will.
Which brokers are allowed?
Any broker regulated by a recognised authority — FCA, CySEC, ASIC, FSCA and the like — that supports MT4 or MT5 and can connect to Myfxbook. We do not name brokers or recommend one. No broker pays us and we are not going to start pointing traders anywhere.
Can you see my password or my trades?
No. You connect your account to Myfxbook yourself, read-only. We never see your password. We read the same public page anyone can read.
Does JYM say I am a good trader?
No. A placement means one thing: you finished top five in this edition, with numbers nobody could edit. We don't vouch for anyone. We don't tell anyone who to follow.
Do I have to promote JYM to my audience?
No — please don't. If you want to mention it, say the facts only: that you entered, when it starts, when results are published. Nothing more.
Why is it free? What do you get?
Edition One makes no money. No fee. No sponsor. No broker pays us. If a broker paid us, the ranking would be worth nothing.
