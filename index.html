FAQ

The questions traders ask.

Why would I join?
Because right now you have no way to prove your trading is real that a stranger will believe. Everyone has screenshots. Everyone has a good month to show. A verified placement is the one thing about your trading that cannot be manufactured — and it costs you nothing, changes nothing about how you trade, and stays private if it goes badly.
What if my three months go badly?
Nothing is published. Only the top five are named, with their return. If you don't place, no result, no rank and no number ever appears.
Can you see my password or my trades?
No. You connect your account to Myfxbook yourself, with read-only access. Your password never comes to us. We read the same public page anyone can read.
Does JYM say I am a good trader?
No. A placement means one thing: you finished in the top five of this edition, over this window, with numbers nobody could edit. We don't vouch for anyone and we don't tell anyone who to follow.
Do I have to promote JYM to my audience?
No — please don't. If you want to mention it, state the facts only: that you entered, the date it starts, and the date results are published. Nothing more.
Is the interview guaranteed?
No. We are trying to arrange one for the top five and nothing is signed. Enter for the verified record, not for this. If it does not happen, we will say so.
Why is it free? What do you get?
Edition One makes no money. No fee, no sponsor, no broker pays us. If money came from brokers, the ranking would be worth nothing — so it does not.
