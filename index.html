FAQ

The questions traders ask.

Can you see my password or my trades?
No. You connect your account to Myfxbook yourself, with read-only access. Your password never comes to us. We read the same public page anyone can read.
What if my six months are weak?
We publish nothing. Only the top five are named with their return. Your name on the entrant list shows one thing — that you agreed to be checked.
Why is it free? What do you get?
Edition one makes no money. No fee, no sponsor, no broker pays us. If money came from brokers, the ranking would be worth nothing — so it does not.
Who can enter?
A live MT4 or MT5 account at a regulated broker. At least $2,000 in it. At least 10 closed trades over the six months. One account, declared before the start. Thirty places, selected — not first come.
